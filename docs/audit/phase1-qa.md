# Map v2 — Phase 1 QA (P1-13)

Run 2026-10-10 on branch `feat/map-v2`. Every number below has a file behind it; `NOT MEASURED` means it was not, and why. Plain statement of where Phase 1 stands: **everything built behind `?map=v2` works and passes its checks, with three honest exceptions** (M1 and M2's skeleton at the 4x CPU profile, the gallery weight target, and M3 as literally written; each has a recommendation in the tracker, decisions D11 and D13), **and the sheet's Back behaviour still needs a real phone.**

## 1. How it was run

- Chromium 1228 through Playwright and the DevTools protocol, GPU on (Intel HD Graphics 5500, `--use-angle=d3d11`; without the flags frame times are about 10x slower, which is how the first M3 baseline was caught).
- Hermetic: `config.js`, MapTiler and `/api/v1/*` are answered by the scripts, the clock is frozen at Saturday 2026-10-10 10:00 in Pretoria, data is a synthetic fixture. Nothing reads the real Supabase project. Real MapTiler tiles were used for the screenshots and pixel samples (a few dozen tile requests).
- Baseline profile (Appendix C): CPU x4, DevTools "Fast 4G" values as recalled (download 1,012,500 B/s, upload 168,750 B/s, latency 165 ms; not read from a specification), 390 x 844 at device scale factor 3, mobile touch.
- Tools: `apps/connect/scripts/map-v2/` (`harness`, `measure`, `measure-sheet`, `m3-compare`, `pin-contrast`, `qa-matrix`, `m7-diff`, `requests-diff`, `contrast`, `lint-frontend`...). Raw output: `docs/audit/data/`. Screenshots: `docs/audit/img/`.

## 6. Limits and findings (stated plainly)

1. **Dark covers the map, pins, labels, sheet, gallery and the Map look menu.** The shared v1 chrome (search row, category pills, Map Key, bottom navigation, MapLibre buttons) stays light; making it dark means changing every screen and belongs with the design reference (RESUME C17). The v1 `EntityCard` body inside a Place or Event sheet sits on a light card in the dark look, because its text is dark.
2. **Part of the v1 map chrome is below 44 px**: the category pills (30.5 px high), their scroll arrows (28 px) and the search input row. Map v2 does not redesign them (Q5, scope); the sheet, pins, Map look button and the MapLibre locate and zoom buttons (raised from 29 px under the flag) meet M6. Listed as a follow-up.
3. **Gallery weight:** stored images are uploaded as they are (median 319 KB). A client-side resize before upload would fix this with no schema change; it is outside Phase 1.
4. **Logo thumbnails:** P1-04 asked for 96 x 96 thumbnails of at most 12 KB; no thumbnail pipeline exists, so logos load at their stored size, only at zoom 15 and above, inside the viewport plus 20 %, or when selected.
5. **Bundle size:** +13.7 KB gzipped for every visitor, flag off included (D14). Loading the Map v2 modules only when the flag is on would remove it, but needs a second hashed bundle in the shared `@citizens/frontend-build` package, so it is a separate decision.
6. **v1 `EntityCard` Share is a stub** (toast only, copies nothing). Flag off is unchanged by design; the sheet's own Share is the real one.
7. **A MapTiler key is written in a tracked document** (`apps/connect/CITIZENS_STRATEGIC_DIRECTION_MAY2026.md`, one line). It is a public browser key (the app ships it in `config.js`), but a repo document should not carry it and the key should be restricted by referrer in the MapTiler dashboard. Not touched here.
8. The tracker and RESUME_HERE said production runs MapTiler `streets-v2`. It runs a custom Dataviz-based style; both documents are corrected in this PR.

## 7. Needs a real phone (Stephen), the Back behaviour first

Android Chrome (mid-range) and iOS Safari. Playwright cannot reproduce Chrome's Back intervention, so everything about Back here is the emulated half.

1. **Back, one press:** tap a pin, the sheet opens; press the phone's Back **once**: the sheet closes and you are still on the map. Press Back again: you leave the app as before (the arrival entry).
2. **Back after dragging:** open the sheet, drag it between peek, half and full, then press Back **once**: it must close (not step down one state, not leave the app).
3. **Back from a photo:** Gallery tab, tap a photo, press Back: the photo closes, the sheet stays; Back again: the sheet closes.
4. **Back from the Map look menu:** open it, press Back: the menu closes and nothing else.
5. **View Full Profile** from the sheet: the profile opens, Back returns to the map with no sheet.
6. **Drag feel:** the sheet follows the finger 1:1, a quick flick moves one state, the map does not pan when the drag starts on the sheet, scrolling at full does not move the map.
7. **iOS:** the sheet clears the notch at full and the home indicator at peek; the edge-swipe back gesture closes it.
8. Share opens the system share sheet; Directions opens the maps app; Call dials.
9. Photo viewer: swipe left and right.
10. TalkBack / VoiceOver: a pin is announced as "name, category", the sheet as a dialog with its title, and a snap change is spoken.
11. Dark look outdoors, and the dark base's labels over real streets.
12. Pan smoothness on a mid-range Android with the 45 or so live pins, light and dark.
