### CCQA-0001 · Open for Phase 2 reproduction

A guest arriving for the first time could miss the path to events, places, contributors and opportunities to serve. Phase 1 produced no new observations about frequency, cause or narrow-viewport impact. The finding retains its original ID and severity in the [register](../../../state/findings.json).

Start Phase 2 with fresh desktop and narrow sessions. Record the initial transition and time to usable controls before refreshing. On failure, capture a sanitized console and network summary, then refresh once and record whether the controls return. If reproduced, diagnose initialization and test a visible loading state and keyboard-accessible recovery. Accept when a single guest selection reaches usable Map, Search and Explore on desktop and narrow viewport, refresh preserves access, and delayed or failed starts offer actionable recovery.
