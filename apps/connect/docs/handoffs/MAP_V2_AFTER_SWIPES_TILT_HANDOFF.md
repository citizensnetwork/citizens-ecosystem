## 2. What the swipe fix is, in one paragraph (so you do not re-derive it)

A scroll container (anything with `overflow` other than visible, including the sheet's scroller at peek and half) ends the `touch-action` chain, so the sheet's `touch-action: none` never reached the content inside it and Chrome took a swipe that began there as a page scroll (`pointercancel`). The fix is one CSS variable, `--mv2-ta`, on the scroller and everything in it (`assets/map-v2.css`): `none` at peek and half; `pan-y` at full while the content is scrolled; `pan-down` at full while it is at the top. **Chrome's pan keywords are inverted against intuition: `pan-down` lets a finger moving UP scroll natively and hands a finger moving DOWN to the sheet's own drag** (found by experiment). `touch-action` is read when a touch starts, so a scroll listener keeps `data-top` on the scroller current (`app/map-v2-sheet.jsx`). A mouse never hands off (its drag selects text). Velocity is sampled from the press, so a very fast flick still counts as fast. A browser without `pan-down` (older iOS) keeps the old behaviour (`@supports`).
