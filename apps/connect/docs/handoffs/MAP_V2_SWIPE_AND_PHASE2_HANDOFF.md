## 2. The new request: swipes (tracker R7, RESUME C22) — do this first

**Stephen's report (Android, 2026-10-14):** after tapping a Contributor's marker the card opens, but it "cannot be swiped up, nor swiped back down after seen". He wants swipes up and down to work in every situation. (He tried a real listing; **its name stays out of every file in this public repo**.)

### 2.1 What the code does today (read 2026-10-14, unchanged)
- `app/map-v2-sheet.jsx`, `onPointerDown` (about line 287): the whole sheet starts a drag, **except** (a) `wide` screens (`(min-width: 768px)`, line 37: a 380 px side panel, no snap points, no drag), (b) a press inside `[data-mv2='scroller']` while the sheet is at **full**, (c) a non-primary mouse button. Move, up and cancel are followed on the `document` (line 305 on). `DRAG_START_PX` 6, `FLICK` 0.5 px/ms, `DISMISS_PX` 40. The pointer is captured only once the drag begins (capturing at press would swallow the clicks on the buttons inside). The handle button is `onClick: cycle` (peek → half → full → half).
- `assets/map-v2.css`: the sheet (line 494) and `[data-mv2='grab']` (line 526) are `touch-action: none`; `[data-mv2='scroller']` is `overflow-y: hidden` until full, then `overflow-y: auto; touch-action: pan-y; overscroll-behavior: contain` (lines 665 to 677). `index.html` sets `overscroll-behavior: none` on `body` (line 82). The photo viewer stage is `touch-action: pan-y` (line 939).
- **Why every current test can pass while a thumb fails:** `e2e/map-v2-sheet.spec.ts` drives the sheet with `flick()` (synthetic `PointerEvent` objects dispatched on the handle) and `drag()` (`page.mouse`). Neither is a real touch, so neither goes through the browser's touch pipeline: `touch-action` is never consulted, and Chrome never gets to take a swipe over for scrolling and send `pointercancel`. They prove the drag arithmetic, not a thumb.

### 2.2 Hypotheses, most likely first (all unproven: reproduce before fixing)
1. **No scroll hand-off.** At full, the scroller owns every swipe that starts on it: a swipe down at the top of the content never collapses the sheet (matches "cannot be swiped back down after seen"), and a swipe up at half or peek on content never expands it first. A bottom sheet normally hands the gesture over at the scroll boundary.
2. **Chrome claims the swipe.** A swipe that starts on a child with its own touch behaviour (a gallery tile, a link, a button, an image's native drag, any element whose effective `touch-action` is not `none`) ends in `pointercancel`, so the drag dies without snapping. `onPointerCancel` resets the transform, so the sheet just stays where it was.
3. **Width, not touch.** A phone held sideways (about 800 to 950 CSS px), a large phone, a foldable or a tablet is 768 CSS px or wider, so it gets the side panel and no gestures. "In any situation" may include this. Open decision **D16** (tracker section 10), recommended: the swipe sheet for any touch screen whatever its width, the side panel for a fine pointer.
4. **Thresholds.** 6 px or 0.5 px/ms may be wrong for a thumb on a dense screen.

### 2.3 Plan
1. **Reproduce with REAL touch.** With Stephen's phone if he is there; otherwise in Chromium with `hasTouch: true, isMobile: true` and CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent` (touchStart, several touchMove with timestamps, touchEnd) or `Input.synthesizeScrollGesture` (`gestureSourceType: 'touch'`). Those honour `touch-action` and send `pointercancel`; `page.touchscreen.tap` alone cannot swipe. Reuse `e2e/support/map-v2.ts` (`mockMapNetwork`, `mockContributorDetail`, `openMap`). GPU flags matter only for frame times.
2. **Write the failing tests first**, in a new `e2e/map-v2-sheet-touch.spec.ts` (acceptance in the tracker, R7).
3. **Fix the cause you find.** A candidate worth testing for the hand-off: while the scroller is at `scrollTop === 0` give it `touch-action: pan-up` (the browser keeps upward swipes for scrolling; a downward swipe reaches the page as pointer events and collapses the sheet), switch to `pan-y` once it has scrolled, and re-evaluate on `scroll`. `touch-action` is read at the touch start, so it cannot change mid-gesture; check this in the Chromium Playwright uses AND on the phone. Other options: non-passive `touchmove` with `preventDefault` at the boundary; driving the content scroll in script (loses native inertia). Pick by test, not by taste. Whatever you choose: at non-full states every child must be effectively `touch-action: none`, `pointercancel` must always leave the sheet on a snap state, and the wide rule follows D16.
4. **Keep what Phase 1 earned:** one `useBackGuard` entry per opening and history entries only inside a tap (`e2e/map-v2-sheet.spec.ts` "every history entry the sheet adds is pushed inside a tap"), only `transform` animates, no scroll chaining into the map, 44 px targets, reduced motion, M1 to M7 (tracker section 1), the Place and Event sheets, the photo viewer.
5. **Real-phone checklist for Stephen** (the Back behaviour first, then each swipe in the acceptance list). Playwright cannot reproduce Android Chrome; this item is not done until a phone has passed it.
